Giant Golem AnimSet 1.45 - Shoulder Hit Root Motion Fix

Fixed horizontal root-motion import for hit_shoulder_left and hit_shoulder_right. Their horizontal translation is now extracted as root motion instead of being baked entirely into the bone pose.

For moving playback, enable Apply Root Motion on the Animator (or consume Animator.deltaPosition in your controller).
For stationary playback, use the existing clips in Animation/Humanoid/Inplace. These retain their in-place motion and original asset GUIDs.

Original FBX animation keys, clip names, timing, Avatar mapping, and unrelated animations are preserved. Existing loop settings are unchanged; configure a one-shot state transition for a single hit reaction as appropriate for your controller.

This package uses Unity Humanoid. The Unreal Engine patch is packaged separately and uses Unreal's Force Root Lock / Anim First Frame settings.
